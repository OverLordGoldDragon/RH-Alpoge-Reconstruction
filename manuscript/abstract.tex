\begin{abstract}
On November 20, 2026, Levent Alp\"oge announced a proof of the Riemann hypothesis, found in collaboration with Claude and released as a Lean development. The Lean and Mathlib communities subsequently certified the formal statement and proof. This manuscript reconstructs the argument in conventional mathematics.

The proof reduces the Riemann hypothesis to a one-sided square-root bound for weighted prime sums. Alp\"oge obtains this bound through a number-field analogue of Stepanov's method. A chain of theta lifts gives increasing divisibility at inert primes, and slope inequalities for the associated hermitian lattices control the competing losses. The manuscript develops the analytic reduction, the local divisibility mechanism and the arithmetic accounting, together with substantial parts of the automorphic, geometric and Galois-theoretic machinery needed to assemble the argument.

The reconstruction is not yet complete. The manuscript isolates the internal statements whose conventional proofs still have to be written and the published inputs whose exact forms still require verification. The goal is a human-readable proof that can be checked independently of the Lean development.
\end{abstract}
