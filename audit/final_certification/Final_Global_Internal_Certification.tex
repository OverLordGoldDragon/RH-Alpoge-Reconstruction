\textbf{VERDICT A: NO LIVE INTERNAL RESIDUALS.}

Every one of the $766{,}788$ declarations reachable from \lean{riemannHypothesis} by exact dependency edges has exactly one status, and none is RESIDUAL. So $\lvert R_{\mathrm{internal}}\rvert=0$. The count rests on the following facts, each checked here.

The verdict is about internal mathematics. It says nothing about the fidelity of the external boundary, which carries open items (Section~\ref{sec:ext}), and Section~\ref{sec:not} lists what it does not establish.

\section{Danger-point checks}\label{sec:danger}

Each item is stated as a proposition with its mechanism. ``Read'' means statement and body read in the archive.

\subsection{\texttt{split\_pair\_generation}}
\lean{split_pair_generation := square_pair_glue square_pair_local}. \emph{Proposition.} For the hermitian pair data of the square at a finite set of split places, local pairs with the required generation property exist at each place, and finitely many local pairs glue to one global pair with those localizations. \emph{Mechanism.} At a split place the unitary group is a general linear group, so the pair is a hyperbolic splitting chosen place by place; the gluing is the Chinese remainder theorem for the finitely many places. Closed. One consumer region, PC4.13.

\subsection{The three torsion-field lemmas}
Let $F\subset\mathbb C$ be a number field, $A/F$ an elliptic curve, $H_d=F(\sqrt{-d})$ and $F(A[12])$ the torsion field over $H_d$.
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
\item \lean{finrank_torsionField_le}: $[F(A[12]):\Q]\le[H_d:\Q]\cdot\lvert\mathrm{GL}_2(\Z/12)\rvert$. Galois over $H_d$ acts faithfully on $A[12]\cong(\Z/12)^2$. Closed.
\item \lean{discField_eq_torsionField}: $H_d(A[12])$ contains a twelfth root of $\Delta$. A cube root of $\Delta$ lies in $F(A[3])$ and a fourth root in $F(A[4])$ (the $x$-coordinates of the torsion points give $\Delta$ as a cube and as a fourth power up to the field), and $\gcd(3,4)=1$ gives $\Delta^{1/12}$. Closed.
\item \lean{dvd_discr_torsionField}: if $A$ has good reduction outside $d$, every prime ramified in $F(A[12])$ divides $6d$ or the discriminant of $H_d$. This is the N\'eron--Ogg--Shafarevich criterion (Serre--Tate 1968, Thm.~1; Silverman, AEC VII.7.1). \textbf{EXTERNAL, new row GC-2.}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{\texttt{ramBijective\_holds}}
\emph{Proposition.} For a split place with residue field $\mathbb F_q$, a frame $\beta$ and a pair $P$, the ramified operator $s_{\mathrm{Op}}=\mathrm{dia}_\varepsilon\circ\mathcal F^*$ (finite Fourier transform for the residual character, followed by the diamond operator of a unit $\varepsilon$) is a bijection of the set of ramified vectors onto itself, and it preserves the ramification predicate. \emph{Mechanism.} Finite Fourier inversion, $\mathcal F^*\circ\mathcal F^*$ is the reflection $x\mapsto -x$ times $q^{\dim}$, so $\mathcal F^*$ is invertible; the diamond operator is invertible with inverse $\mathrm{dia}_{\varepsilon^{-1}}$; both commute with the ramification condition, which is a support condition. Read. Closed.

\subsection{Fock reading, vacuum and moved vacuum}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
\item \lean{gaussianTwistedRealReading_entire_and_injective}: the Fock reading $\xi_N$ of a holomorphic theta vector is entire, and $\xi_N(f)=0$ forces $f=0$. Entirety is termwise from the Gaussian factor; injectivity holds because the reading is determined by its values on the dense image of $E$ in the finite adeles and theta vectors are continuous there. Closed.
\item \lean{vacuum_character}: under a Weil datum, $(g,h)$ in the archimedean maximal compacts acts on a theta vector that is a Fock vacuum at every archimedean place by a scalar built from $\chi_S,\chi_T$, $\eta^{-\mathrm{rk}S},\eta^{-\mathrm{rk}T}$ and the determinant roots $x,y$ of $g,h$. Uniqueness of the vacuum line of the Heisenberg representation (Stone--von Neumann) makes the action scalar; the scalar is read on determinants. Closed.
\item \lean{om_pureTensorO}: the Weil action intertwines pure tensors built from Cartan data $s,B$ with those built from the conjugated data $s',B'$. The creation operators are conjugated by the archimedean components. Closed.
\item \lean{vac_moved}: moving the standard vacuum by $(g,h)$ in the finite level groups gives a vacuum for the moved lattices, whose reading lies in the theta space of $\Lambda_C(N,N')$ and is nonzero. Vacuum uniqueness for the moved lattice and injectivity above. Closed.
\item \lean{heisO_finRat_sign}: for self-dual lattices and a standard character, a rational Heisenberg translate in the lattice acts on the lattice vacuum by $(-1)^k$ with $k=\mathrm{Tr}_{E/\Q}(\xi_0((1+\sqrt{-d})/2)^2 h(v,v))\in\Z$. Characteristic of the lattice theta function. Closed.
\item \lean{gaussianReading_finiteRationalHeisenbergTranslate}: the reading turns a finite rational Heisenberg translate into the operator $U$ of the corresponding embedded element. Closed.
\end{enumerate}
