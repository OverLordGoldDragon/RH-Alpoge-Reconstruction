## Two standards that must not be conflated

The project uses two different closure standards, and from the middle of the Final Six Wave the same report can carry both.

- **Strict (formal-descent) standard.** A theorem is closed when its Lean proof body has been read down to classical leaves or to a named published statement. An unread body is a residual, even if its mechanism is known. All syntheses up to `Post_Closure_Synthesis` use this standard, and so do Six Wave 4 (main verdict), Six Wave 5 (main report) and Six Wave 6.
- **Human-proof (proposition) standard.** Introduced on 22 Nov at the user's instruction to Agent 2. A package is closed when it can be restated as ordinary propositions (statement, mechanism, named external inputs, downstream use) with no genuinely bespoke mechanism left unexplained. Unread formal implementation of a known mechanism is listed but not counted as a residual. Six Wave 1 and Six Wave 3 give their verdicts at "the human-proof boundary". Six Wave 5's Proposition Package and Six Wave 4's proposition table apply this standard. Six Wave 2's final verdict also applies it.
